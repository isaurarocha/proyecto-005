# Cadence

A course platform where learners buy video courses and work through their lessons.

## Language

### People

**Admin**:
A person who runs the Cadence platform itself, including approving or declining Refund Requests.
_Avoid_: using "Admin" for a Team Manager

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask to get their money back for a course they bought, reviewed by an Admin and either approved or declined. A purchase can have at most one Refund Request, and a decline is final.
_Avoid_: Cancellation, Chargeback, Reversal, Payback

**Refund**:
The money returned when a Refund Request is approved: exactly the amount paid, never the list price. An approved Refund ends the access that purchase gave, including every Seat of a Team Purchase.
_Avoid_: Chargeback, Reversal, Payback

**Refund Window**:
The 14 days after a purchase during which a Refund Request may be made.

### Teams

**Team Purchase**:
A purchase of several Seats in one course, made by a Team Manager for other people. The buyer does not get access to the course themselves.
_Avoid_: Team licence, bulk purchase

**Seat**:
One person's paid access to a course, bought as part of a Team Purchase and waiting to be claimed.
_Avoid_: Coupon, coupon code, licence

**Seat Link**:
The shareable link a Team Manager sends to someone so they can claim a Seat.
_Avoid_: Coupon link, coupon code

**Cancelled Seat**:
A Seat that no longer gives access to anyone because its Team Purchase was refunded. A Cancelled Seat can never be claimed.
_Avoid_: Refunded Seat, revoked Seat

**Claim**:
To take a Seat for yourself, which gives you access to its course. A Seat can be claimed once.
_Avoid_: Redeem, use, mint

**Team Manager**:
The person who makes Team Purchases and hands out their Seats.
_Avoid_: Team admin

**Team**:
A Team Manager together with their Team Purchases. People who claim Seats are learners, not members of the Team.
